# Layout Preflight Rules

These rules are heuristics, not universal design laws.

## Universal checks

### Text fidelity
Compare against approved copy. Flag missing, added, modified, or duplicated names, figures, dates, words, or punctuation. Never silently shorten.

### Hierarchy
Establish one primary focal point and clear reading order. Keep supporting content visibly subordinate. Avoid competing headlines.

### Readability
Assess text at actual viewing scale. Flag small text, poor contrast, dense paragraphs, and text on busy images.

### Spacing
Use consistent margins and aligned content groups. Check edge clipping, crowded CTAs, baseline mismatches, and awkward empty space.

### Rendering risk
Flag intricate logos, long text embedded in generated imagery, complex multi-panel layouts, tiny labels, overlays, and pixel-exact requirements. Recommend native typography when fidelity matters.

## Format rules
Instagram carousel: consistent dimensions, margins, hierarchy, and pacing across slides; one main idea per slide when feasible.

Quote post: dominant quote, secondary attribution, protected line breaks, uncluttered background.

One-page PDF: clear title and sections, readable contact details, printable margins, no unexpected clipping.

Announcement: prioritize what happened, when, and what to do; make important dates scannable.

Slides: readable titles, consistent footer/alignment, low paragraph density, cross-slide consistency.

Certificates: exact recipient names and dates, hierarchy among award, recipient, issuer and signature, printable boundaries.

## Severity
BLOCKER: essential information missing or purpose defeated.
MAJOR: substantial readability or hierarchy issue.
MINOR: non-critical polish issue.
UNVERIFIED: requires inspection or unavailable source.

## Response
State readiness, top issue, specific remedy, and next action. Do not manufacture issues.
